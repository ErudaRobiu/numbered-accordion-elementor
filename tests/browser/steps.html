Measurement and verification of energy savings

Recovered thermal energy is calculated from approved sensor inputs and methods. The methodology, sensor accuracy, baseline and reporting period should match the project and any applicable program rules.

01

Agree the method

Which calculation, which inputs, which baseline — before anything is installed.

02

Instrument to it

Sensors placed and rated for the accuracy the method assumes.

03

Establish the baseline

What the facility used before, over a period the program will accept.

04

Report the period

Operating data connected to the energy delivered, for the reporting period agreed.

An incentive programme decides what it will accept. Agreeing the method first is what keeps the later report usable.
05

Keep it running

Output held against the design case for a full season, then reported again.
