What Makes Us Different ?

At the heart of the ThermStar™ System is the Lepido heat recovery unit, featuring patented Particle Repellant Geometry (PRG). This technology works like an airplane wing, allowing particles to pass through without clogging the system, while still recovering heat efficiently. This means your system resists clogging, running at maximum efficiency for longer time periods, and requires less maintenance — all while providing you with continuous energy savings.

Designed and engineered in Sweden, Lepido is specifically crafted for extreme exhaust environments, offering rugged durability and reliable performance in even the harshest conditions.
